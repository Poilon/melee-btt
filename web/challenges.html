    WINDOWS 10 / 11 · 64-BIT

    Get TTRC Dolphin Beta

    Download the ZIP, extract it, and open TTRC Companion.vbs. The companion runs independently of Dolphin.

    Download for Windows ↓
    1. Set up your session. Choose music and rumble settings in the companion, then click Launch Dolphin. Click Open and select your original Melee USA 1.02 ISO. It stays in its current folder.
    2. Keep the companion open. Your runs and replay history stay here, even after closing Dolphin. Use Quit companion when you are done.
    3. Sign in from the companion. Create an account with a username and password, then confirm the connection code. Choose your character in Melee and play.
    Already running? Open companion ↗

    Release notes and checksums ↗

    GameCube adapters may need their Windows driver installed. The ISO is not included. Replays are saved next to Dolphin. The replay player downloads on first use. New personal bests upload privately with their replays.
