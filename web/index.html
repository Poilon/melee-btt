READY WHEN YOU ARE

From your browser to your first clear

  1. Open the game

    Select Play in browser on desktop Chrome or Edge. Choose a character and press Break the Targets.

  2. Connect your controller

    A standard gamepad connects automatically. For a GameCube controller, download the connector from the game's Controller panel and follow its setup instructions. Keyboard controls are available too.

  3. Sign in and play

    Use Sign in in the game to create an account or use your existing Melee BTT account. Finish a stage to save your time and replay. Your personal best and its replay appear here automatically.

  4. Watch, share, practise

    In Your saved runs, choose Watch to replay a run or Share to make it watchable by others. You can also download the .slp, step frame by frame, or take control to practise.
