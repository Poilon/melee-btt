WINDOWS 10 / 11 · 64-BIT

Get TTRC Dolphin

Download the ZIP, extract it, and open Slippi Dolphin.exe. The companion starts with Dolphin.

Download for Windows ↓
  1. Choose your ISO in Dolphin. Click Open and select your original Melee USA 1.02 ISO. It stays in its current folder.
  2. Open Tools → TTRC Companion. Your runs and settings open in your browser. There is no separate launcher to keep open.
  3. Sign in from the companion. Continue with Google, choose your TTRC username, and confirm the connection code. Choose your character in Melee and play.
