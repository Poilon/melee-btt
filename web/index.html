Install Custom Melee BTT Beta

You need your own Super Smash Bros. Melee USA v1.02 ISO. Custom Melee BTT includes Dolphin, the companion and the level editor. You do not need a separate Slippi installation.

Your original ISO stays unchanged. Custom Melee BTT builds its own playable copy on your PC.

Download Custom Melee BTT Beta for Windows ↓Custom-Melee-BTT-Windows-x64.zip · complete portable packageRelease notes & checksums ↗

Download the Windows ZIP above, not the “Source code” archives on GitHub. The Melee ISO is not included.

From download to your first run

  1. Extract the whole ZIP

    Right-click Custom-Melee-BTT-Windows-x64.zip and choose Extract All…. Put the extracted Custom Melee BTT folder somewhere you can write to, such as your Desktop or Games folder.

    Keep every file and subfolder together. Do not run Dolphin from inside the ZIP, or move just the executable out of the folder.

  2. Open Dolphin and select your ISO

    Inside the extracted folder, double-click Slippi Dolphin.exe. The setup window asks for your original Melee USA v1.02 .iso. Click Choose ISO…, wait for the checksum verification, then click Launch game. Dolphin stays in setup until the ISO is verified.

    There is no special folder to put your ISO in and no manual Gecko code to paste. Custom Melee BTT prepares the 26 levels automatically. A loading window shows the current step and elapsed time until Dolphin opens; the first launch builds the levels, and later launches reuse them. The complete ISO checksum is checked on every launch. If the file is moved, missing or modified, the setup window asks you to select a valid ISO again.

  3. Set up your controller

    In Dolphin, open Controllers. For a supported GameCube USB adapter, set Port 1 to GameCube Adapter for Wii U. For another controller or a keyboard, choose Standard Controller, then Configure and bind the buttons.

    If your adapter is not detected, follow Dolphin’s adapter setup guide ↗, then reopen Dolphin.

  4. Connect your Custom Melee BTT account

    In the game’s Stadium menu, select Log in. Your browser opens the Custom Melee BTT sign-in page. Create a username and password, or sign in to your existing Custom Melee BTT account.

    Once the browser confirms the connection, return to the game. The menu shows Logged as your username. The companion connects to the same account automatically. You can also start sign-in from the companion; both stay connected after restarting. No code to copy. This is a Custom Melee BTT account; your Slippi login is separate. Sign in before a run you want to submit.

  5. Play Target Test

    Select Target Test in Stadium, choose a character, and break all ten targets. The published Custom Melee BTT levels load automatically. Press Start, then Z to restart a level.

    While signed in and connected to the internet, eligible completed records and their replays are submitted automatically. Open Leaderboard in Stadium to check times and watch replays. The first replay automatically installs the official replay player, with a download progress window. Later replays reuse it. The website shows the same published leaderboards.

Settings & level editor

Open Custom Melee BTT Companion.vbs from the same folder for music, rumble and other settings, or to open the level editor. Close the running game before changing game settings.

To use the online editor with Test in Dolphin, keep your local companion running. It launches the Dolphin bundled with that companion. Editor playtests are separate from the published leaderboards.

Next time & updates

Open Slippi Dolphin.exe again. Custom Melee BTT remembers your ISO and account.

Updates download in the background and install after Dolphin closes. Your account, settings, ISO and replays are kept. Close and reopen Dolphin to use an installed update.

If something does not work

Dolphin will not open, or a DLL is missing

Extract the entire release again into a new folder, then launch Slippi Dolphin.exe from that folder. Check that runtime, Sys and the DLL files are beside it. Downloading just the executable is not enough.

My ISO is rejected

Custom Melee BTT requires the original USA v1.02 Melee ISO. PAL, Japanese, other revisions and already modified ISOs will not work. Choose your original, unmodified .iso when Dolphin asks.

I signed in, but the game still says “Not signed in”

Keep Dolphin open while signing in. Start the connection from Log in in that running game or Sign in in its companion, finish the browser page, then return to Dolphin. Allow a few seconds for both to update. Signing in on an unrelated browser tab alone does not connect the game.

The online editor cannot find Dolphin

Open Custom Melee BTT Companion.vbs from your extracted Custom Melee BTT folder first. Select your Melee ISO through Dolphin at least once, then retry Test in Dolphin. The website connects to your local companion; it does not search your PC for emulators.
