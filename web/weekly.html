How it works

  1. Open the competition and sign in with your Melee BTT account.
  2. Play the featured character on its original Target Test stage. Try as often as you want; your fastest eligible clear counts.
  3. Finish and upload before the countdown ends. Runs must start during this competition. Previous records and late uploads do not carry over.
  4. Your best weekly time and replay appear here automatically. Equal times share a rank.
  5. Every Monday at 00:00 in Europe/Paris, the next competition opens. Completed competitions keep their results and replays.

Official C-stick camera controls only; replay takeovers are practice and do not count. UCF on or off is accepted. The game version is fixed for each competition and loads automatically.

The opening round runs from October 10 to October 12, 2026. Following rounds last a full calendar week.

Community beta competition: browser-recorded runs are not independently verified or approved for external competitive leaderboards. See the leaderboard rules and Dolphin setup guide.
